Multi-device control · Team

Worship leader, preacher, operator —
each controls their own screen.

In the Team edition the worship leader runs the Stage screen from their tablet — chords and cues for musicians. The preacher controls their own display. The operator runs the Hall from the computer. Three roles, three devices, all over Wi-Fi.

  • Stage screen — worship leader's tablet: chords & cues for musicians
  • Preacher screen — speaker's tablet: their personal display
  • Hall screen — operator's computer: lyrics for the congregation
  • All connected over Wi-Fi — no extra cables between roles
  • Works with any combination of monitors & projectors

Chords & Modulation

Every song in
any key, instantly.

Songs are stored with chords positioned above the lyrics. The worship leader decides the key — DisplayWord transposes everything in real time. Mid-song modulation is a single + or − button.

Special Cues

Instant signals
to your musicians.

During worship the operator sends cues to the stage screen — A Cappella, Drums Only, Repeat, Quiet — as bright banners that appear for a few seconds. The congregation never sees them.

  • Pre-built cues with icons and colors
  • Custom cues: text, colour, up to two icons — the cue editor is built in
  • Two modes: a banner over the current slide, or a banner that hides the slide
  • Accent mode (Ctrl-click) — a stronger delivery; works for quick messages too
  • Configurable cue sets for different parts of the service and different screens

Song Library

All your songs,
organized and ready.

Store your full song catalog with lyrics, chords, keys, and notes. Multiple song books for different communities or languages. Build a service setlist in minutes.

  • Multiple song books (communities, languages)
  • Full song editor with chord placement
  • Search by song title and lyrics; filters by community, song book, vocalist, tags and favourites
  • One-click setlist building — click to add, click again to remove
  • Print chord sheets as PDF for musicians
